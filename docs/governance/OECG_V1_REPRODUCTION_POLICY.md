# OECG V1 reproduction policy

A reproducible work unit binds:

- source/input revision or digest;
- operator/code version;
- deterministic/random seed policy where applicable;
- environment/provider assumptions;
- exact command/workflow;
- observed output digest/receipt;
- tolerance/metric when equality is not exact.

`same narrative != reproduced result`

A fixture replay proves only its declared reconstruction level. Reproduction of a semantic structure is not automatically reproduction of scientific or physical behavior.
