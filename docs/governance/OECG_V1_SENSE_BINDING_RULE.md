# OECG V1 sense-binding rule

A lexical surface may have multiple occurrences and multiple context-bound senses.

A sense binding needs:

- the occurrence/source reference;
- namespace/context;
- sense authority reference;
- sense version;
- semantic state;
- correction/supersession pointer when changed.

Ambiguity => fork/provisional or TOKEN_VAZIO; do not overwrite history to manufacture one canonical meaning.

`SAME_SURFACE != SAME_SENSE`
