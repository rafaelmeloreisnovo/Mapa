# OECG V1 uncertainty policy

Uncertainty is typed by source and reducibility:

- `EPISTEMIC`: evidence/knowledge incomplete;
- `MEASUREMENT`: observable has finite resolution/error;
- `MODEL`: assumptions/parameters/model class unresolved;
- `PROVIDER`: external platform state/runtime unavailable;
- `AUTHORITY`: source/authorship/sense/destination authority unresolved;
- `PRIVACY`: applicability/authorization/retention boundary unresolved;
- `REPRODUCTION`: fixture/operator/version/execution not closed.

Reduce uncertainty with the narrowest authoritative probe. If the probe is unavailable, preserve TOKEN_VAZIO and stop the dependent promotion.

Do not lower an uncertainty score merely by deleting or relabeling unresolved records.
