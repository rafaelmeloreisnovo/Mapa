## Non-authority

OECG V1 does not:

- change producer repositories;
- alter repository rulesets, secrets, permissions or external provider configuration;
- establish authorship merely because an occurrence has `actor=USER`;
- transform a faith statement into a scientific or legal claim;
- infer consent, child-safety status, privacy status or legal basis from missing information;
- claim statistical Six Sigma capability;
- authorize autonomous goal creation;
- turn CI success into global compliance or safety PASS.
