## 2. Contributor ownership

## 3. Copyright license grant

Contributor grants the Project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable (except for uncured material breach where applicable), transferable and sublicensable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, distribute, and otherwise exploit the Contribution as necessary to:

- include it in RAFAELIA;
- license RAFAELIA under public research/non-commercial terms;
- license RAFAELIA under open-source terms when applicable;
- grant separate commercial licenses;
- preserve/archive/reproduce provenance and evidence.

Counsel must review the scope before adoption.

## 4. Patent license

To the extent Contributor owns or controls patent claims necessarily infringed by the Contribution as submitted, Contributor grants a patent license only in the scope expressly stated in the final executed CLA.

This section remains `TOKEN_VAZIO_COUNSEL_REQUIRED` until a final patent clause is approved.

## 5. Representations

Contributor represents, to the best of Contributor's knowledge and subject to the final signed language, that:

- Contributor is legally entitled to submit the Contribution;
- Contributor has disclosed third-party material;
- Contributor has not knowingly removed required copyright/license notices;
- Contribution provenance supplied to the Project is materially accurate;
- employment/contractual restrictions affecting the Contribution have been disclosed.

## 6. Third-party material

Contributor must identify any material not wholly authored/controlled by Contributor and provide:

- source;
- license;
- version/ref;
- attribution/NOTICE;
- modification description.

## 7. Attribution

The Project may credit Contributor using reasonable contribution metadata.

Nothing in the CLA permits false attribution or removal of third-party credit.

## 8. No automatic authorship transfer

```text
PULL_REQUEST != COPYRIGHT_ASSIGNMENT
COMMIT != WAIVER_OF_AUTHORSHIP
CLA_ACCEPTANCE -> ONLY_RIGHTS_EXPRESSLY_GRANTED
```

## 9. No obligation to use

The Project is not obligated to accept, merge, distribute, or commercially exploit a Contribution.

## 10. Records

Acceptance of a final CLA should produce an auditable record identifying:

- CLA version;
- Contributor;
- acceptance timestamp/method;
- contribution scope;
- repository;
- signatures or provider evidence.
